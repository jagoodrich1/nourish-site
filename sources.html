Places

Restaurant locations, names, addresses and hours come from OpenStreetMap, © OpenStreetMap contributors, under the Open Database License. See openstreetmap.org/copyright.

Menus

Menus are read from what restaurants publish. Each one names the site it came from and the day it was read. This app is not connected with any restaurant or chain, and every name and trademark belongs to its owner.
