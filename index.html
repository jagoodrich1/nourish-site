Nourish: Allergy Menu Finder

Nourish finds restaurants near you and reads their menus against what you avoid: allergens, ingredients and diets. It says a dish contains something when the menu names it, and free of something only when the menu or a sealed product’s label says so. Always ask the restaurant before you order.
