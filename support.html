Support

Write to nourishmenus.help@gmail.com. We aim to answer within a day.

A menu or an answer is wrong

Open the restaurant in the app and use Report a problem under its menu, or write to us with the restaurant’s name and town and what is wrong. Until you hear back, please ask the restaurant itself before you order: a menu can be out of date, and a kitchen can change a recipe.

You run a restaurant

If your menu here is wrong, or you would like it taken down, write to nourishmenus.help@gmail.com with the restaurant’s name and address. This app is not connected with any restaurant or chain.

A comment

You can report a comment, or block the person who wrote it, from the comment itself. You can delete your own comments at any time. If you have lost the phone that posted something, write to us and tell us where it is.

Your data

Settings, Your data, in the app lets you export everything the app holds about you, and delete everything, including your comments and reports on our server. The privacy policy lists exactly what goes where.
