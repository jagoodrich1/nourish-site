Privacy policy

Updated 3 November 2026

The short version

There is no account, no advertising and no analytics. Nothing is sold. Your allergies, diet, goals and meals stay on your phone. Comments you choose to post are public. The rest of this page lists exactly what goes where.

On your phone only

Your filters and allergies, the name, age, weight and height you enter, your goals and logged meals, favourites, the town you typed, how often you open places, who you have blocked, and crash notes. None of this is sent to us. Deleting the app removes it, and so does Settings, Your data, Delete everything.

On our server

Comments and reports you choose to send, with the place they are about and a random number this app made up when it was installed. That number is not your name or your email, and it is how we know a comment is yours so that you can delete it. A display name is optional. If you report a menu or a comment, that report goes privately to the person who checks them.

Our server is run by Supabase. Like any server it sees your internet address when the app connects, which happens when you open a menu we hold there, read comments, look at the map, or start the app.

Others, while you use the app

Our own server is asked for the squares of the map around you, to find restaurants there. OpenStreetMap’s map servers (overpass-api.de and overpass.openstreetmap.fr) are told the area of the map you are looking at, to find stores and airports, and restaurants if our server has no answer.

The US Department of Agriculture’s FoodData Central and Open Food Facts are told what you search for and barcodes you scan, to look up nutrition. They are not told what is on a menu you open.

Apple draws the map, under Apple’s own privacy policy. If you tap Directions, your phone opens Apple Maps with that restaurant as the destination.

Each of them sees your internet address when your phone asks. If you open a restaurant’s website or a delivery link, you are on their site and under their policy.

Your location

Location is only used while the app is open, to show places near you. We do not store it, and it does not reach our server. You can say no and type a town or a ZIP instead, which is answered on your phone.

The camera

The camera reads barcodes. Reading a photo of a menu is not available in this version, and no picture leaves your phone.

Health information

Allergies, diets and meals can be health information, which is why they stay on your phone. We do not collect them, share them or sell them. The only way anything about your health could reach us is if you write it in a comment or a report, and a comment is public, so please think before you do.

Children

This app is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect anything from them.

Your choices

Settings, Your data lets you export everything the app holds about you, and delete everything, including your comments and reports on our server. If you have lost the phone that posted something, write to nourishmenus.help@gmail.com and tell us where it is.

We keep a comment until you delete it or we remove it, and a report for as long as we need it to look after the app.

Changes and contact

If this changes in a way that affects you, the app will tell you. Questions go to nourishmenus.help@gmail.com.
